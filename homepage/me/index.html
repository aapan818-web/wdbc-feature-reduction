Hi, I'm Gavin.

I'm Gavin Pan, a first-year master's student in Global Health at Peking University, with an undergraduate background in Preventive Medicine. I play basketball for PKU's second team and flag football for PKU DRAGONS.

Off the court, I'm into strength training, climbing, skiing, swimming, and badminton. I love exploring new places, photographing mountains, coastlines, and city streets, and spending time with my cat, Lemon. If you're into sports, travel, or cats, we'll have plenty to talk about.
